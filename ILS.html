Cleaning

Cleaning is the removing of dirt, dust, crumbs, and germs from surfaces
or objects. Cleaning occurs in many different contexts, and uses many
different methods.

Types of Cleaning Services

  • Daily Cleaning
  • Deep Cleaning
  • Clinical Cleaning
  • Window cleaning
  • Curtain Cleaning

Tips in Cleaning

  • Clean the whole place not just a part of it
  • Clear the clutter
  • Sweep then mop
  • Wipe mirrors and glass
  • Regularly Clean your cleaning tools

Sanitization

Sanitizing is a process of cleaning certain area or surface
in such a manner so that it is made bacteria-free and
elementally clean all types of microbes and viruses that
can infect the human body and cause different kinds of diseases.
Sanitizing is the next step after the cleaning process.

Types of Sanitizing Services

  • Antimicrobial Sanitization
  • Foam Sanitization
  • Chemical Sanitization

Tips in Sanitizing

  • Wipe down high-touch surfaces
  • Use appropriate products for different kinds of surfaces
  • Read and follow the instructions on the sanitizing product
  • Sanitize products you buy before storing
  • Routinely sanitize things you use

Disinfection

Disinfecting is the reduction in the number of bacteria, viruses, or fungi to
a desired concentration. It describes a process that eliminates
many or all pathogenic microorganisms, except bacterial spores,
on inanimate objects.

Types of Disinfecting Services

  • Vehicle Disinfection
  • ATP Testing
  • Misting
  • Thermal Disinfection

Tips in Disinfecting

  • Focus on the areas that gets touched or used a lot like doorknobs
  • Regularly disinfect
  • Follow the instructions on the disinfecting product label
  • If an area is not visibly dirty, you must still clean it
  • Use disinfecting wipes when cleaning electronics
